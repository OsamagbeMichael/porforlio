# Formats and ideas

## 10 formats that keep working
Pick one before filming; it decides the shots. "Here" is what this editor already does for it.

| Format | What it is | Here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talking head | You, to camera, one idea | the default `Reel` |
| Listicle | "3 mistakes", "5 tips" | `labels` per item, `sketch-note`, `step-card` |
| Ranking | Rank things worst to best; people stay for the top | `tier-list` |
| Screen share | Show the real steps on screen | `broll` screenshots/recordings, `laptop-reveal`, `scroll-showcase` |
| Voice-over b-roll | Talk over clips you already have; for off-camera days | `broll` over the whole take |
| Green screen | A post, stat or headline behind you, your take in front | `paper-desk`, `footage-text`, `text-behind` (needs `npm run cutout`) |
| Split screen | Two things side by side to compare | `before-after` |
| Reaction | Someone else's clip, then your opinion | `broll` (keep their clip short and credited) |
| Clone | Two of you arguing ("me vs my brain") | film twice, same framing; ask Claude to stack the takes |
| Yap | Walking or casual, one strong opinion | the default `Reel`, `captionStyle: "serif"` |

## Core values: don't chase a new subject every day
Pick **3–4 core ideas** your page stands for and keep coming back to them, each time with a different format, hook or visual. New topics every day make a page lose direction; the same few ideas told many ways build a brand people recognise. Write yours in `docs/signature-style.md`.

## 350 ideas from one niche
One main niche → 5 sub-niches → 10 formats (above) → 7 angles = 350 combinations.
The 7 angles: tutorial, comparison, myth or common mistake, do vs don't, quick tip, transformation (before → after), challenge.

To fill a month, ask Claude:
> My audience is [who]. The problem they want solved is [what]. List 50 small, specific moments where they *feel* that problem (things they do, say or check), ranked from "most people" to "few people". Then turn the top 10 into reel ideas, each with a format and an angle from `docs/formats.md`.

Moments beat topics: "refreshed the analytics three times" is a hook; "growth tips" is not.

## Posting every day without burning out
- Spend at most **1 hour a day** on content: talk, add b-roll and captions, post. Skip fancy effects on daily posts.
- **Batch on Saturday**: write and film the whole week in 2–3 hours, then only edit on weekdays.
- If that's still too much, price your hour and pay an editor with part of it.
