# Hooks

The first sentence decides whether people keep watching. Add a pattern when a reel does well, and note its views.

## Patterns
1. **Exact method.** "Here's exactly how I [outcome] with [tool]."
2. **System in N seconds.** "This is my whole [process] in 60 seconds."
3. **Proof first.** "Every [thing] you've seen from me was made by [tool]." Show the proof on screen.
4. **Mistake.** "Stop [common habit]. Do this instead."
5. **Number list.** "[N] things I wish I knew before [goal]." Pairs well with section labels.
6. **Confession.** "I have to admit something about [topic]…" or "I was wrong about [thing]."
7. **The test.** "I tried [thing] for [N] days so you don't have to." Show day 1 and the last day.
8. **Unpopular opinion.** "Unpopular opinion: [claim]." Then the reason in one line.
9. **Beginner door.** "If you're new to [topic], start here." Narrow, so the right people stay.
10. **Hype check.** "I expected to hate [thing]. I didn't." / "Is [thing] worth it? Here's after a month."
11. **Save it.** "Save this for the next time you [situation]." Works on lists and checklists.
12. **Nobody says this.** "Nobody tells you this about [topic]." Only if it's true and specific.

Fill the blank with the most specific thing you can say: a number, a tool, a place. "3 things" beats "some things".

## Rhythm (applies to the whole script)
Mix long sentences with short ones of **five words or fewer**. All-long sounds like a lecture; long, short, long, short sounds like a person.
> "I had to find a library that stays open all night, and the first one I tried had a problem. **It was closed.**"

## Writing rules (the script is 90% of the video)
1. **Open with a comparison.** Two options side by side ("smart editor vs lazy editor", "this vs that") pulls attention fastest.
2. **Example before explanation.** Tell a tiny story first ("two people walk into a market; one brings a bag…"), then explain. The more it matches the audience's real problem, the better it lands.
3. **Join sentences with "but" or "therefore", never "and then".** "I went freelance, **but** I couldn't pay rent, **therefore** I joined a community…" keeps tension going.
4. **A sign-off slogan.** The same short phrase at the end of every reel. Regular viewers wait for it, which lifts watch time. Pick yours once, put it in `docs/signature-style.md`, and keep it.

## Loop ending
End on a line that runs straight back into the first line, so the reel replays without a seam and the rewatch counts.
Film the "loop part" first, then build the hook from it. Example: last line "…and that's why I never", first line "edit at night."
When a reel loops, say so in `storyboard.md` so the last cut isn't trimmed.
